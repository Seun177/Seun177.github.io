Privacy policy

Last updated 1 November 2026

Sivyrz ("the studio", "we") is a personal content-planning tool run by one creator on their own computer. This policy explains what data it uses and how.

Who uses the studio

The studio is used only by its owner. It has no public sign-ups and doesn't collect data from members of the public.

Data we access

  • Pinterest: after the owner signs in with Pinterest and grants permission, the studio reads the owner's boards and pins (images, titles, descriptions and links) using the read-only scopes boards:read and pins:read, plus user_accounts:read to identify the account.
  • Other connected accounts: the owner may connect their own social media accounts to read their own post statistics (followers, views, likes, comments, shares).

How we use it

Data is used only to build the owner's private mood board, suggest outfits and plan videos. We don't use it for advertising, profiling, or training models.

Storage and security

Data and access tokens are stored locally on the owner's computer. They're not uploaded to any server we run. Tokens are never published or shared.

Sharing

We don't sell, rent or share Pinterest data with anyone. Image descriptions may be sent to an AI service the owner uses, solely to describe outfits for the owner's own planning.

Retention and deletion

Data is kept only as long as the owner needs it for planning. Disconnecting Pinterest in the studio deletes its stored tokens, and access can be revoked at any time in Pinterest settings under Security and logins → Apps. To ask for any data to be deleted, use the contact below.

Children

The studio isn't directed at children and doesn't knowingly process children's data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top will be updated.
