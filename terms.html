2. Who can use it

Sivyrz runs on the owner's own computer and is used only by its owner. By connecting an account, the owner confirms they are the holder of that account and are allowed to give the app access to it.

3. Connected accounts

Sivyrz can connect to the owner's own social media accounts (such as TikTok, Instagram and Pinterest) through each platform's official API. It requests read-only access to the owner's own profile information and content statistics, such as follower counts, video views, likes, comments and shares. It does not post, edit or delete content, and it does not access other people's accounts.

4. Acceptable use

The app is used in line with the terms and developer policies of each platform it connects to. It is not used to scrape other users' data, to spam, to automate engagement, or to do anything that breaks a platform's rules.

5. Your data

Data pulled from connected accounts is stored locally on the owner's computer and is used only for personal performance review. It is not sold, shared or published. See the Privacy Policy for details. Access can be removed at any time by disconnecting the account inside the app or from the platform's own app permissions settings.
