A planning studio for menswear content.

Sivyrz is a personal tool used by one creator to plan outfits and short-form fashion videos.

What it does

  • Collects outfit inspiration from the creator's own Pinterest boards into a private mood board.
  • Compares that inspiration with clothes the creator already owns to suggest new outfits.
  • Turns approved outfits into video ideas, shot lists and edit plans.
  • Shows the creator's own post performance so they can see what's working.

Who it's for

Sivyrz isn't a public service and has no sign-ups. It runs on the creator's own computer and only connects to accounts the creator owns.

Pinterest

With the creator's permission (Pinterest sign-in), the studio reads boards and pins from the creator's own account. It only reads: it never posts, edits or deletes anything on Pinterest, and it never touches other users' data. See the privacy policy for details.
